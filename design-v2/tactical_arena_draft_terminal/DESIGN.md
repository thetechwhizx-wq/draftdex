## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-stakes, hyper-tactile draft war-room and tactical battle terminal. Designed for real-time Pokémon auctions, roster optimization, and competitive arena operations, it fuses the kinetic energy of an arcade broadcast HUD with the laser-precision of modern developer telemetry.

The aesthetic balances precision sci-fi brutalism with elevated tactile ergonomics:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep void space, low-reflectance navy chassis, punctuated by electric voltage yellow strikes and status neon indicators.
- **Surface Quality:** Crisp, high-contrast, mechanical card architectures with subtle interior beveling, tactile borders, and strict 16px geometric alignment.
- **Emotional Intent:** Urgent, commanding, calculated, and high-performance. It channels the tension of an auction countdown clock ticking down alongside the analytical rigor of competitive meta-tier drafting.
- **Physicality:** Hardware-grade tactile surfaces, recessed stat readouts, physical switch micro-interactions, and instant-response state changes designed for rapid decision-making under time pressure.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated dark-mode hierarchy engineered to mitigate eye fatigue while delivering immediate chromatic differentiation during rapid draft bid cycles.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#FFE033` / `#FFD000`):** Electric Voltage Yellow. Reserved for dominant calls-to-action, active bid leadership, current turn focus, and auction hammer highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF`):** Cyan Neon. Utilized for tactical radar telemetry, tier rankings, system telemetry, and special move synergies.
- **Tertiary & Urgent (`#EF4444`):** Hazard Warning Red. Denotes countdown expiration (<5 seconds), outbid alerts, stamina depletion, and critical hazards.
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy:** Built on dark navy layers rather than desaturated charcoal to preserve game vibrancy:
  - Canvas Base: `#0B111E`
  - Structural Panels & Dividers: `#111A2E`
  - High-Contrast Card Enclosures: `#162238`
  - Interactive Surfaces & Hover States: `#1E2D4A`

### Elemental Taxonomy
The 18 canonical Pokémon elemental types operate as an immutable global token set. Type tokens are strictly utilized for badge chips, stat category borders, move classifications, and effectiveness badges, maintaining instant visual recognition without clashing against primary UI chrome.

## Typography

Typography unifies tournament broadcast drama with tactical cockpit clarity.

- **Headings (`Space Grotesk`):** Engineered with geometric angularity and tech-forward apertures. Used in upper and sentence cases for stage banners, auction headlines, team callouts, and auction clock timers.
- **Body & Controls (`Geist`):** Delivers clean readability at small sizes, optimal line density, and a utilitarian feel across rosters, ability descriptions, and logs.
- **Athletic Tabular Readouts:** Numeric values (Base Stat Total, HP, Attack, Speed, Currency Points, Bid Clocks) utilize `Space Grotesk` configured with monospaced tabular figures (`tnum`) and uppercase styling to ensure digits remain fixed in space during countdown micro-animations.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal is structured around an uncompromising 16px spatial grid rhythm (`space-md`). 

- **Grid Discipline:** 12-column layout on desktop viewports, refactoring into an asymmetrical split-pane cockpit (Auction Stage + Live Bidding Log / Draft Queue). Tablet switches to 8 columns; mobile collapses to a single focused 4-column column with fixed-bottom auction action sheets.
- **Rhythm Rules:** All layout heights, paddings, and card gaps increment through the base-4/base-8/base-16 cadence. Micro spacing between stat chips and icons locks to `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px). Structural containment between draft blocks locks to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- **Touch & Ergonomics:** All interactive buttons, chip selectors, and bid increments adhere to a strict minimum hit dimension of 44px × 44px (with 48px preferred on primary bidding controls) to eliminate misclicks in high-speed auction environments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic muddy shadows. Instead, it relies on tactile physical layering, inset chamfers, and edge-lit luminosity.

1. **Base Layer (L0 - Ground):** `#0B111E` flat cockpit backdrop. Often patterned with an ultra-faint 16px isometric dot grid overlay at 4% opacity.
2. **Structural Chassis (L1 - Panels):** `#111A2E` framed with a 1px border of `#223555`. Flat elevation, isolating workspace modules.
3. **Card Enclosures (L2 - Roster & Bidding Modules):** `#162238` framed with a 1px solid `#354E7A` border. Ambient drop: `0px 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
4. **Active Focus & Hover (L3 - Active Card / Nominated Target):** `#1E2D4A` with a 1px accent stroke:
   - Default active: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` border with `0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)` cyan perimeter aura.
   - Live auction focus: `rgba(255, 224, 51, 0.5)` border with `0 0 16px rgba(255, 224, 51, 0.25)` voltage bloom.
5. **HUD Overlays & Modals (L4):** `#111A2E` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and surrounded by a dual-stroke pseudo-beveled frame (`1px solid #FFE033` interior edge, `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)` outer shadow).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 Roundedness** (`0.5rem` / 8px radius standard, `1rem` / 16px for card frames), striking a distinct balance between retro-arcade hardware and modern esports cockpits.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `rounded-lg` (16px) creates disciplined, robust silhouettes.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Inputs:** `rounded` (8px) provides a sturdy, industrial, tactile push-button presence.
- **Type Pills & Stat Indicators:** Maintain rounded pill caps or structured clipped badges with 4px internal corner radii, reinforcing high-density arcade metadata tags.
- **Chamfer Accents:** Select hero frames and timer displays may utilize a micro 45-degree corner notch on top-right borders via clipped pathing, amplifying the sci-fi arena diagnostic theme.

## Components

### 1. Action Buttons & Bidding Triggers
- **Primary (Bid / Nominate):** Background `#FFE033`, text `#0B111E`, font `Space Grotesk` 700 bold uppercase. Height: 48px. Crisp 8px border radius. Hover state transitions to `#FFD000` with a subtle `0 0 14px rgba(255, 224, 51, 0.4)` outer voltage glow. Active press translates down `1px` to simulate physical switch depression.
- **Secondary (Pass / Quick Action):** Background `#162238`, text `#00F0FF`, border 1px solid `#00F0FF`. Hover transitions to background `#1E2D4A` with `0 0 10px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Urgent / Hazard (Surrender / Forfeit / Drop):** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, text `#EF4444`, border 1px solid `#EF4444`.

### 2. Pokémon Draft & Auction Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#162238`, 1px border `#354E7A`, 16px corner radius, internal padding of 16px.
- **Header:** Pokémon sprite/render positioned against a muted radial gradient reflecting its primary type color, framed by species name (`Space Grotesk` 600) and draft point cost in athletic tabular numerals.
- **Stat Strip:** Recessed dark bar (`#111A2E`) showcasing Base Stat Total (BST), HP, ATK, DEF, SPA, SPD, SPE. Stats scale via horizontal segmented energy meters with neon fill thresholds.

### 3. Type Chips & Badges
- **Tokens:** Rendered using the 18 explicit elemental color tokens.
- **Execution:** Filled pill badge with bold white text (`label-badge`), accompanied by a 15% opacity elemental drop-glow. Border is 1px solid with 30% white opacity for arcade contrast against dark surfaces.

### 4. Form Inputs & Steppers
- **Bid Input:** Monospaced 20px `Space Grotesk` numerals centered within an inset container (`#0B111E` background, 1px `#223555` border, active focus border `#FFE033`).
- **Quick-Bid Steppers (+10, +50, Max):** Segmented button row directly beneath input, minimum target 44px × 44px, tactile feedback on toggle.

### 5. Checkboxes, Toggles, & Filter Chips
- **Checkboxes:** 20px × 20px squares with 4px border radius. Inactive: `#111A2E` with 1px border `#354E7A`. Checked: `#FFE033` fill with an angled black checkmark.
- **Tier Filter Badges (Uber, OU, UU, RU, NU):** Dense toggle chips that illuminate with neon borders matching tier severity when active.

### 6. Timer & Telemetry Readouts
- **Auction Countdown Clock:** High-contrast digital readout in `Space Grotesk` bold. Normal state (>10s): White. Warning state (5s–10s): `#FFE033`. Critical urgency (<5s): Flashing `#EF4444` with synchronized audio-visual pulse.
